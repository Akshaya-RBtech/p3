About WasteZero

WasteZero is an intelligent, AI-driven hostel food management platform. The application predicts precise daily meal attendance, calculates exact ingredient quantities to prevent preparation waste, and actively connects students with admins for immediate feedback and dietary adjustments.

Key Features

We provide advanced analytics leveraging machine learning (XGBoost), predictive modeling, smart scheduling, automated semantic feedback tagging via Gemini AI, real-time vote collection, and seamless mobile responsiveness across Android, iOS and Web.

Contact & Support

Need help or have an inquiry? Reach out to the hostel administration or email us at support@wastezero.com. Let's make our campus greener, together.
